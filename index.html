Create your key

A secp256k1 keypair will be generated by the local node. The private key is encrypted on disk; the passphrase is never stored.

If you lose both the passphrase and encrypted backup, the wallet cannot be recovered. DeroM is currently a development network and wallet sending is not implemented.

Self-hosted solo mining

The node builds jobs directly from its local chain state. It has no pool accounts or share payouts. Difficulty adjusts toward one block every five minutes; after a fast block, the first few follow-up blocks may still arrive quickly as difficulty catches up.
